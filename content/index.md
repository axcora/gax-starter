## GAX Starter Kit

**This is not a framework. This is a starting point.**

GAX Starter gives you a clean, minimal, and fast foundation to build anything static - personal blog, project documentation, product landing, or portfolio.

No hardcode. All data lives in your Markdown frontmatter and `_data/` folder. Templates are just `.gax` files - simple, readable, and powerful.
